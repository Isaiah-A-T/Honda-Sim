Materials and what's estimated

Published: 86 × 86 mm bore and stroke, 11.0:1, 139 mm rods, 22 mm pins, 48 mm rod big end, powdered-metal stock rods. Estimated: part masses and sections, material grades, the cylinder pressure and piston temperature model, and every limit. Load is demand ÷ capacity. Below 80% a part lives indefinitely; above it, fatigue builds up and "life used" climbs; at 150% it breaks at once.
